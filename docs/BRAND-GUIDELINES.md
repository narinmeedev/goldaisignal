# Gold AI Signal — Brand Control v1.0

## Brand name

- Official name: **Gold AI Signal**
- Short name: **GA**
- Domain: **goldaisig.com**
- Descriptor: **XAUUSD Decision Intelligence**

Do not use `GoalAiSignal.com` unless the product and domain are formally renamed.

## Logo concept

The open **G** represents continuous market monitoring. The structured **A** represents AI-assisted analysis. The restrained signal pulse across the A represents explainable market data—not a promise that price will always rise.

## Logo files

- `ga-mark.svg`: primary gold master mark with a subtle controlled gradient.
- `ga-mark-flat.svg`: flat production mark for favicon, embroidery, stamping, and small sizes.
- `ga-wordmark-dark.svg`: horizontal lockup for dark backgrounds.

## Typography

- Thai and UI: **Kanit**, weights 400–600.
- Prices, timestamps, symbols, and technical data: **Geist Mono**.
- Avoid extra-bold condensed fonts in body copy; they make financial information harder to scan.

## Clear space and minimum size

- Keep clear space around the mark equal to the width of its gold stroke.
- Minimum digital size: `24 px`; use the flat mark below `48 px`.
- The horizontal wordmark should not be displayed below `180 px` wide.

## Approved usage

- Gold mark on Canvas or Surface.
- Flat black or white mark for one-color production.
- Social avatar: centered mark at approximately 58% of the canvas width.

## Do not

- Stretch, rotate, outline, or add a drop shadow/glow.
- Place the gold mark on yellow, orange, or visually noisy imagery.
- add crowns, coins, flames, robots, or profit arrows to the master mark.
- Use claims such as “guaranteed profit” or “100% win rate” next to the brand.
